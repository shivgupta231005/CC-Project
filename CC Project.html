About Us

Shiv Shakti Sweet Shop offers traditional Indian sweets, bakery items, namkeen and milk products for everyday cravings and festive occasions.

From barfi and ladoo to cakes, samosas and fresh paneer, our counter is made to bring familiar flavours to your table.
